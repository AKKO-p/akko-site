About AKKO

AI Runtime for regulated European workloads.

AKKO is the runtime layer that orchestrates, governs, audits and optimises private LLMs on infrastructure you own. Six proprietary components on top of an open-source Lego foundation. Built for banking, healthcare and the public sector, regulated environments where every prompt, every token and every row must stay inside your perimeter.

Our model: Open Blueprints + Open Lab + Closed Runtime

AKKO is not a single-license product. The foundation is open, the lab is open, the runtime is commercial. This is the honest model that lets regulated buyers audit what they install, while the proprietary engine keeps your prompts and governance under a signed contract.

📦 Open Blueprints

Apache 2.0. Helm charts, Terraform modules, agent templates and integration glue. Public on GitHub, free to fork and audit.

🧪 Open Lab

Apache 2.0. 30+ notebooks across banking, healthcare and retail. Lab kernels, Spark Connect examples, dbt marts, sample governed pipelines.

🔒 Closed Runtime

Commercial license. 17 SQL AI functions, NL2SQL orchestrator, governance engine, observability layer, catalog manager, control plane.

Why we built AKKO

Regulated organisations across Europe were asked to choose between two bad options: send their data to a US hyperscaler to get modern AI, or freeze on legacy on-prem stacks with no AI story at all. AKKO exists because that trade-off is no longer acceptable.

We started from a layered platform position (Compute, Query, Catalog, Storage, BI, Lab, AI, Governance, Observability) and built the missing pieces — orchestration, AI governance, audit receipts, SQL AI functions — into a runtime you can install on your own Kubernetes. No phone-home. No foreign API call. Your keys, your data, your perimeter.

Who AKKO is for

  • Banking — fraud detection, AML pipelines, regulatory RAG, SQL AI for fact tables under DORA.
  • Healthcare — patient cohort analytics, governed knowledge bases for clinical staff, fully on-prem.
  • Public sector — sovereign analytics, citizen-facing portals, NIS2-aligned audit trail.

Three verticals, one runtime, one governance model. Notebook-friendly for data scientists, SQL-friendly for analysts, contract-friendly for procurement.

What stays open, what stays closed

We will keep the blueprints and the lab open under Apache 2.0 so the community can inspect, fork and contribute. We keep the runtime commercial because that is what funds the engineering work that makes AKKO worth installing in a regulated environment in the first place. We say it out loud so prospects know what they are buying — and what they are not.
